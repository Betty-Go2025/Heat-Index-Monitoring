DepEd Institutional Directives & Protocols

DepEd Order No. 037, s. 2022
In-Person Classes Directive
AUTOMATIC SHIFT TO ALTERNATIVE DELIVERY MODES (ADM)
🚫

As heat index reaches or exceeds 42°C, in-person classes are suspended. School heads are mandated to immediately transition to Modular Distance Learning, Online Learning, or Blended Modalities to protect learners and school personnel.

🏃
Outdoor & Physical Activities
STRICTLY PROHIBITED

All flag ceremonies, physical education (PE) sessions, sports training, and outdoor assemblies are cancelled.

💨
Classroom Environment
Maximize Air Circulation

Open all windows and doors for cross-ventilation. Ensure electric fans are positioned for optimal airflow. Transfer classes from upper/tin-roof rooms.

👕
Uniform & Attire Relief
Relaxed Uniform Policy

DepEd permits students and teachers to wear comfortable, light-colored clothing or school PE uniforms in place of heavy regular school uniforms.

💧
Hydration Protocol
30-Min Water Breaks

Teachers must instruct mandatory hydration breaks every 30 minutes. School water stations must be verified and operational.

Authority: School Head / Principal Discretion & LGU Coordination

🩺 DepEd DRRMS & DOH Heat Illness First-Aid Matrix

Standard operating procedures for school clinic personnel, clinic teachers, and class advisers when learners or staff manifest heat-related illnesses.

Mild Warning 🦵

1. Heat Cramps (Pulikat)

Symptoms:
  • Painful muscle spasms (legs, arms, abdomen)
  • Heavy sweating during activity
  • Mild dizziness or fatigue
First-Aid Actions:
  • Move learner to shaded, well-ventilated area
  • Gently stretch and massage cramped muscles
  • Provide cool water or electrolyte solution in small sips
  • Do NOT resume heavy physical activity
Recovery Time: 30 - 60 minutes
Moderate Alert 🥵

2. Heat Exhaustion

Symptoms:
  • Heavy sweating with cold, pale, clammy skin
  • Fast, weak pulse & shallow breathing
  • Nausea, vomiting, headache, fainting
  • Normal or slightly elevated body temp
First-Aid Actions:
  • Transfer immediately to air-conditioned clinic
  • Loosen tight clothing, remove socks and shoes
  • Apply cold, wet towels to neck, armpits, and groin
  • Sip cool water slowly if fully conscious
  • If vomiting occurs, position on their side
*If symptoms worsen or last >1 hour, call for medical transport!
Critical Emergency 🚨

3. Heat Stroke

Symptoms:
  • High body temp (≥ 40°C / 104°F)
  • Hot, red, dry skin (or heavy sweat in collapse)
  • Rapid, strong pulse
  • Confusion, slurred speech, seizures, loss of consciousness
Emergency Protocol (Call 911!):
  • IMMEDIATE EMERGENCY: Dial 911 / Red Cross 143
  • Rapid cooling: immerse in cool water or cold shower
  • Pack ice / cold compress to neck, armpits, groin
  • Do NOT force fluids if person is unconscious
  • Notify parents and School DRRM Officer immediately
⚠️ Medical Emergency: Can be fatal within minutes!
